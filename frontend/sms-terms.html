SMS Opt-In & Messaging Consent

EverythingShul Ticket System — tickets.everythingshul.com

How it works: Customers initiate all SMS contact themselves by texting the event's published phone number. We never send unsolicited messages. All SMS is transactional — sent only in direct response to a customer-initiated text for the purpose of purchasing event tickets.

How Customers Opt In

The phone number for SMS ticket ordering is displayed on the event's public ticket sale page at tickets.everythingshul.com. By texting that number, the customer is explicitly and voluntarily requesting the ticket ordering service. No prior account, signup, or form submission is required — the act of texting the number constitutes opt-in.

What Messages We Send

  • Responses to customer-initiated ticket ordering requests
  • Order confirmation with ticket details
  • Payment processing status
  • Replies to HELP requests

We do not send marketing messages, promotional blasts, or any unsolicited outbound SMS.

Sample Message Exchange

Customer → Hi
System → Welcome to Beth Israel Annual Gala ticket ordering! Reply 1 for Adult ($75) or 2 for Child ($35). Reply STOP to cancel.
Customer → 1
System → Great! How many Adult tickets? (1-10)
Customer → 2
System → 2x Adult = $150. Your name?
... continues through payment ...
System → Payment confirmed! Your tickets have been emailed to you. Thank you!

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies per transaction. You will only receive messages while actively placing a ticket order. Once the order is complete or cancelled, no further messages are sent unless you initiate a new conversation.

Contact

For questions about this service, contact us at support@everythingshul.com or visit tickets.everythingshul.com.
