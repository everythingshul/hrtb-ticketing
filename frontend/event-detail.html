⚠️ Duplicate Attendees Found

What this means: The file you're uploading contains people whose phone number or email already exists in your attendee list.

For each duplicate, choose one of three options:
  Update existing — keep the existing ticket & ID, but update their table/seat/name with the new data from your file.
  Keep as-is — do nothing. Skip this person, keep everything exactly as it was.
  Add as duplicate — add them again as a brand new attendee with a new ticket ID, even though they already exist.
